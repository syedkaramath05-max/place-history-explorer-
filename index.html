Evidence Lab: compare your own sources

Paste text from an inscription reading, an old map legend, a gazetteer or any document. Add two or more sources, then compare. The AI builds a timeline using only what your sources show.

Answers are generated by Claude from its knowledge of books, inscriptions, gazetteers and palm-leaf (ōlaichuvadi) literature. It cannot open those documents live, so dates and names marked "low confidence" must be checked against the sources listed. Photos and live old maps are not embedded here; use the archive links under the map.
